# 📊 Marketing Funnel and Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Marketing Funnel and Conversion Performance Analysis is a Data Analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior throughout the website conversion funnel.

The project analyzes user journeys, traffic sources, device usage, engagement patterns, cart activity, and purchase behavior to identify conversion bottlenecks and opportunities for business growth. Based on the findings, strategic recommendations are provided to improve conversion rates, optimize customer experience, and increase revenue.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer behavior across the marketing funnel
- Measure conversion performance at different funnel stages
- Identify drop-off points in the customer journey
- Evaluate traffic sources and device performance
- Understand cart and purchase behavior
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 📈 Key Insights

### Conversion Performance

- Only **5,050 customers** completed a purchase out of **12,719 total visitors**.
- A significant number of visitors left without adding products to their cart.
- Most users dropped off before reaching payment confirmation.

### Device Analysis

- Mobile devices generated the highest traffic.
- Desktop users showed the highest purchase rate.

### Referral Source Analysis

- Google generated the highest website traffic.
- Google also produced the highest number of purchases.

### Country-wise Performance

- France recorded the highest number of purchases.
- Australia recorded the lowest purchase activity.

### Cart Behavior

- Customers with more items in their carts were generally more likely to purchase.
- Users with only two items in their cart had the lowest purchase rate.
- A large number of purchases occurred without significant cart activity, indicating direct purchase behavior.

### User Engagement

- Most visitors spent their time on the Home Page.
- The Confirmation Page had the lowest engagement.
- Customers spending excessive time on the website were less likely to convert.

### Monthly Trends

- March 2025 recorded the highest number of purchases.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business should:

✅ Improve the website funnel to reduce customer drop-offs.

✅ Optimize the mobile user experience to improve mobile conversions.

✅ Increase investment in Google marketing campaigns.

✅ Enhance product discovery and navigation on the Home Page.

✅ Use personalized recommendations to encourage cart additions.

✅ Expand marketing efforts in high-performing countries.

✅ Improve engagement and conversion in low-performing regions.

✅ Simplify the checkout process to increase completed purchases.

✅ Use retargeting campaigns for visitors who leave without purchasing.
